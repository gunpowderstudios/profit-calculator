How to read this

VAT

Normally VAT is removed from the selling price to get your real ex-VAT revenue. Tick “Ignore VAT” when you deliberately want the entered price treated as ex-VAT / zero-rated for modelling.

Profit before overheads

This is the money left from each sale after the game costs and selling costs have been taken off. It is not the final company profit because it still has to help pay wages, marketing and the other general costs of running the business.

Distributor discount

Enter the total discount the distributor is asking for. Delivery is entered simply as a cost per game, with 15p used as the starting estimate.

Cash reserve

This is money you may want to keep back to replace the stock you have sold. We show it separately so you can see both the sale profit and the cash you want to protect for reordering.

Preset game costs are based on the latest Gardners profitability figures supplied by Gunpowder Studios. Amazon's UK Toys & Games referral fee default is 15%; FBA, storage, advertising and other fees should be replaced with current Seller Central figures for each SKU.
