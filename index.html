How to read this

VAT

VAT is removed from the selling price to get your real ex-VAT revenue. Reclaimable VAT on business costs is not treated as a profit cost.

Contribution

This is what remains after the product and sales-channel costs entered above. It still has to help pay salaries, marketing, software and other company overheads.

Distributor discount

Do not add retailer and distributor margins again if the quoted discount already represents the total discount from RRP.

Cash reserve

Keep restocking cash visible, but separate it from the accounting contribution so you can see both profitability and cash-flow safety.
